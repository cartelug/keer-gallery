Shipping & Delivery

Pickup

Artwork may be collected directly from the gallery by arrangement. Contact us to schedule a pickup time.

Local Delivery (Kampala)

Local delivery within Kampala is available. Costs are confirmed after reviewing the artwork size, framing, and location.

National Delivery (Uganda)

National delivery is available across Uganda. Costs and timelines are confirmed after order placement.

International Shipping

Available for select works. Costs vary by destination and artwork size or weight. Contact us for a shipping quote before ordering.

Framed Artwork

Framed pieces require special packaging and may incur higher delivery costs. We take great care to ensure safe delivery.

Delivery Timeline

All artwork is delivered only after full payment is received. Commission timelines are confirmed during the order process.
