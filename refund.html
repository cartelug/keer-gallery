Refund Policy

Original Artworks

Due to the nature of original artworks, all sales are final. If an artwork arrives damaged, contact us within 48 hours with photographic evidence.

Print Orders

Prints may be eligible for a refund or replacement if received damaged. Contact us within 48 hours of receipt.

Commission Deposits

Commission deposits are non-refundable once artwork creation has begun. If you cancel before work starts, a partial refund may be issued at our discretion.

Damaged Delivery

In the event of damage during delivery, we will work with you toward a resolution, which may include replacement or partial refund.

Custom Work

Custom commissioned artworks are non-refundable as they are created specifically for you.
