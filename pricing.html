Reservation & Deposits

Artwork is reserved only after payment confirmation. If payment is not completed within the agreed time, the artwork may be released for sale again.

Commissions: A 50% deposit is required to begin work. The remaining 50% is due before delivery.

Payment reference: Please use your name and artwork title as the payment reference. Orders are confirmed after payment verification.
